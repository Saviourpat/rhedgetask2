Frequently Asked Questions

What are the prerequisites for the BootCamp?

Basic knowledge of [relevant field] is helpful, but no prior experience is required!

How long is the program?

The program lasts for 3 weeks, with 5 sessions per week and stand-up meetings every day at 10 AM.

What happens during the daily stand-up meetings?

The daily stand-up meetings at 10 AM are quick 45-minute sessions where participants provide updates on their progress, discuss any challenges, and receive guidance from mentors.

How long is each session?

Each session is approximately 1.5 to 2 hours long, depending on the topic and interaction with participants.

Is there post-BootCamp support?

Yes, we offer career coaching, interview prep, and access to a network of professionals even after you graduate!

What is the format of the program?

The program combines live sessions, hands-on exercises, and one-on-one mentorship to help you build practical skills in your chosen field.

Do I need to attend every session?

While attendance is highly recommended for maximum learning, we understand that life happens. However, consistent participation will ensure you get the most out of the program.
